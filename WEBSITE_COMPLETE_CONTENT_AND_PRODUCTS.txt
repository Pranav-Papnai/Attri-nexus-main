CUSTOM PROCESSING & MILLING FINISHES (CHOSEN PER VARIETY):
1. Stone Ground (Chakki Grinding):
   * Method: Traditional slow stone chakki grinding between two heavy natural stones.
   * Technical Profile: Dheere peesne ki wajah se friction se zyada heat (<40°C) produce nahi
     hoti, jisse wheat ke natural nutrients, vitamins aur oils (germ) preserve rehte hain.
   * Best For: Authentic soft, sweet household chapatis, phulkas & health-conscious retail.

2. Roller Milling (Modern Method):
   * Method: Industrial continuous high-speed steel roller reduction and plansifter sieving.
   * Technical Profile: Wheat ko steel ke bade-bade rollers ke through guzaara jata hai. Isme
     grain ke alag-alag parts (bran, germ, aur endosperm) ko easily separate kiya ja sakta hai.
   * Best For: Refined flour (maida), high-ratio bakery flour, and uniform commercial white/brown flours.

3. Impact / Hammer Milling:
   * Method: High-velocity hardened metal hammers/blades pulverizing grains via intense kinetic impact.
   * Technical Profile: High-speed metal hammers ya blades wheat grains par zor se impact daal kar
     unhe todte aur peeste hain.
   * Best For: Coarse whole meals, high-fiber compounding, and livestock animal feed rations.

4. Pin Milling:
   * Method: High-speed counter-rotating pin discs generating high-velocity particle collision.
   * Technical Profile: Rotating pins wale discs wheat particles ko aapas mein ya pins ke saath
     takra kar aur baarik (fine) powder mein convert karte hain.
   * Best For: Exceptionally uniform, fine, silky flour powders for premium industrial confectionery & baking.
